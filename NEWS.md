# FuzzyRandom 0.1.0
- Initial release.
- Level-wise representation of fuzzy numbers and samples of fuzzy random
  variables.
- Level-wise moments: `expectation()`, `variance()`, `covariance()`,
  `correlation()`.
- Level-wise empirical distribution function `fuzzy_ecdf()`.
- Level-wise goodness-of-fit via `ks_test()` (one-sample and two-sample).
- Fuzzy normal distribution `fuzzy_normal()`, level-wise CLT `clt()` and LLN
  `lln()`.
- Simulation with `rnorm_fuzzy()`.
